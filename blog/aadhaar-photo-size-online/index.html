How to update your Aadhaar photo

Corrected 18 November 2026 · 3 min read · Govt Forms

Your files never leave your device
Ad

You cannot change the photograph on your Aadhaar by uploading a resized JPG online. UIDAI directs people to an Aadhaar enrolment or update centre for photograph and other biometric updates. See UIDAI’s official update guidance.

Correcting our earlier guide

Our previous page incorrectly described an online photo-upload process and an official pixel/KB preset. We have removed those instructions and relabelled the image settings as a general-purpose portrait preset. A browser photo resizer cannot update your Aadhaar record.

How to arrange a photo update

  1. Check the current process on UIDAI’s official website.
  2. Locate an enrolment or update centre and check its appointment arrangements.
  3. Visit the centre for the photograph update. Follow the operator’s instructions and retain your acknowledgement.

For centre services, use UIDAI’s Aadhaar Seva Kendra information. Check current fees and document requirements there; this site does not book appointments or submit updates.

Can a centre operator use a photo resizer?

Yes. An operator can prepare an existing portrait for a separate form or office workflow that accepts an uploaded image. Our 200×230 photo resizer remains available for that purpose. It is not a replacement for the official capture process, and its dimensions are not an official UIDAI specification.

A document upload is a different task

A separate application may ask you for a copy of an identity document or a portrait. Preparing that file does not change the photograph held by UIDAI. Read that application’s own instructions before cropping or compressing anything.

If a separate form explicitly accepts an image upload, our image resizer and image compressor can help meet its stated limits. They do not verify identity, guarantee acceptance, or replace an official biometric capture.

Keep private information private

Do not send Aadhaar numbers, OTPs, identity scans or personal photos to ToolShoppy support. Image processing in our tools takes place on your device. For an Aadhaar service, use the official UIDAI channels rather than a third-party photo service.
