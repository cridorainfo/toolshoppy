UAE passport photo specifications

UAE passport, Emirates ID, and visa applications require a standard ICAO-format photograph measuring 35 × 45 mm (approximately 413 × 531 pixels) with a maximum file size of 300 KB in JPEG format on a plain white background.

The photo must show a clear front-facing view with a neutral expression, both eyes open, and no glasses with thick frames. Head coverings are permitted for religious reasons but must not obscure facial features. The photo should be recent — taken within the last 6 months.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Keep a neutral expression, both eyes open, with no heavy shadows.
  • Head coverings for religious reasons are generally allowed, but the face must remain fully visible.
  • Always check the issuing authority’s current notification before you submit. Specifications change, and the preset is a starting point.
  • Use a recent photo. Authorities often reject old ones.

What is the UAE passport photo size?

UAE passport and Emirates ID applications require a 35×45mm photograph at 413×531 pixels, max 300KB, with a white background.

Is UAE photo size the same as UK passport photo?

Yes. Both UAE and UK use the standard 35×45mm (413×531 px) passport photo format, though file size limits may differ slightly by portal.
