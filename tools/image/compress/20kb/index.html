What 20 KB means for an image

20 KB is about 20,480 bytes, roughly one hundredth of a typical phone photo (2–5 MB). Reaching it means cutting the file by about 99%, which a compressor does by lowering JPEG quality and, for large pictures, shrinking the pixel dimensions. That is why a 20 KB file is usually small on screen too, often a few hundred pixels wide.

Some pictures compress far better than others. A face against a plain background, a signature on white paper or a simple stamp can look fine at 20 KB. A busy scene full of detail, or a photo with small text, will look soft or blocky.

What to expect at 20 KB

Kind of imageWhat to expect at 20 KB
Signature or stamp on a white backgroundUsually fits easily and stays readable.
Passport-style portrait (plain background)Often fits at a small pixel size; check the face is still clear.
Phone photo of a person at a distanceWill be reduced a lot; detail and background become soft.
Photo of a document with small printUsually unreadable at this size. Use a higher target.

These are general expectations. The result depends on the picture, so always check the downloaded file.

Tips for a clear 20 KB image

  • Crop to the face or signature first with the image cropper. Less empty space means better quality per kilobyte.
  • Use the largest, sharpest original you have; compressing an already-small copy hurts more.
  • Photograph signatures with dark ink on plain white paper in good light.
  • If a form states a range such as “10–20 KB”, check the result is above the minimum as well. The tool aims for as close to 20 KB as it can without going over.
  • If a form also needs exact pixel dimensions, resize first with the image resizer, then compress.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compress an image to 20 KB?

Open the tool, add your image, keep the 20 KB preset selected and press Compress. It searches for the best JPEG quality that stays under 20 KB, reduces dimensions if needed, and shows the actual size before you download.

Will my image still look good at 20 KB?

It depends on the picture. Simple images such as signatures and plain-background portraits usually stay clear. Detailed photos become softer, so look at the result at full size before uploading.

Can I get exactly 20 KB?

The tool gets as close as it can without going over, but an exact byte count is not guaranteed. Check the size shown after compression against your form’s limit.

My form says 10 KB to 20 KB. What should I do?

Compress to 20 KB, then check the downloaded size. If it is below the minimum, use the 50 KB preset or a custom target between the two limits.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The compression runs in your browser, so your photo or signature is never sent to ToolShoppy.
