How to compress an image to 200 KB

  1. Select your photo or scan, drop it on the box, or paste it with Ctrl+V.
  2. The 200 KB target is already selected. Pick a different preset or a custom value if your limit is different.
  3. Press Compress. The tool searches for the best quality that stays under the target.
  4. Check the compressed size is under 200 KB, then download the image or a ZIP for several files.

What 200 KB means for an image

200 KB is about 204,800 bytes: large enough to keep good detail in a portrait or a readable scan of a certificate or form, yet small enough for most upload fields and for email. A phone photo of 3 MB needs to shrink by about 93%, which mostly comes from lowering quality and trimming the pixel size rather than from heavy blur.

It is a good target when 50 KB looks too soft and the portal allows more, and a sensible default for photographed documents.

What to expect at 200 KB

Kind of imageWhat to expect at 200 KB
Portrait or ID photoTypically clear, with good skin and hair detail.
Photographed document (single page)Usually readable; keep the page flat and well lit.
Landscape or product photoGood for web and email, not for large prints.
Very large camera photo (20 MP+)Scaled down substantially; keep the original for printing.

These are general expectations. The result depends on the picture, so always check the downloaded file.

Tips for a clear 200 KB image

  • Photograph documents flat, in daylight and square to the camera. Shadows and angles waste kilobytes and hurt legibility.
  • Select several pages or photos and download the ZIP to compress them in one go.
  • If you need a single file from many pages, combine them with Image to PDF first.
  • Check dimensions too: some portals set minimum and maximum pixel sizes as well as a KB limit.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compress an image to 200 KB?

Add the image, keep the 200 KB preset, press Compress and download it. For several images, download all of them together as a ZIP.

Is 200 KB enough for a readable document photo?

Usually yes for a single, well-lit page. For dense small print or multi-page documents, check the result at full size or compress a PDF instead.

What if the result is still over 200 KB?

Very detailed images may need smaller dimensions. Resize the image first, then compress; or choose a smaller target. The page shows the actual size so you can check.

Does it work with HEIC photos from an iPhone?

Convert HEIC to JPG first with the image converter, then compress the JPG here.

Do my files leave my device?

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
