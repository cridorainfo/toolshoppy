PAN card photo specifications

Permanent Account Number (PAN) card applications through NSDL and UTIITSL online portals require a square passport-style photograph at approximately 213 × 213 pixels (2.5 cm × 3.5 cm) with a maximum file size of 100 KB in JPEG format.

The photo must have a plain white background, show a clear front-facing view of the applicant, and be recent. Blurry images, selfies with filters, or photos with coloured backgrounds are commonly rejected during online PAN application. The PAN Card preset below is already selected — just upload your photo.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Use a plain, light background and no hats or heavy shadows.
  • Keep your face centred and fully visible.
  • Always check the issuing authority’s current notification before you submit. Specifications change, and the preset is a starting point.
  • Keep the original so you can regenerate the photo if the portal rejects it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PAN card photo size?

PAN card applications on NSDL and UTIITSL portals typically require a square photograph around 213×213 pixels (2.5cm × 3.5cm) with a maximum file size of 100KB in JPEG format.

What background is required for PAN card photo?

PAN card photos require a plain white background with a clear, front-facing view. Coloured or patterned backgrounds are rejected by the portal.
