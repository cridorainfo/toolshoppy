SSC Photo Resizer

The Staff Selection Commission's online application forms (CGL, CHSL, MTS, and others) require a photo of 200×230 pixels, under 50KB — a tighter file-size limit than most other government forms. This page pre-selects the SSC preset so the output fits under 50KB without visible quality loss.

Frequently asked questions

What is the exact SSC photo size?

200×230 pixels, under 50KB, on a plain white background.

My photo keeps coming out blurry at 50KB — why?

50KB is a tight limit. The tool automatically balances JPEG quality against file size — very high-resolution source photos compress more cleanly than already-low-quality ones, so start from the clearest original you have.

Does this cover SSC CGL, CHSL and MTS forms?

The 200×230px / 50KB spec is shared across most SSC recruitment forms, but always confirm against your specific notification.
