How it works

  • Uses Baidu PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 — open-source Chinese OCR, converted to ONNX for the browser
  • Inference runs locally via WebAssembly; your photo is never uploaded
  • Model caches after first download so later runs work offline
  • Great for receipts, invoices, ID cards, and screenshots

Free offline image OCR — no signup

Upload a photo and ToolShoppy runs PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5 (Baidu’s open-source OCR) entirely in your browser. After the first model download, recognition can continue from cache without sending images to a server.

How to extract text from an image

  1. Upload a clear photo or screenshot.
  2. Wait for on-device detection & recognition (first visit downloads the model).
  3. Copy the text or download a .txt file.

Frequently asked questions

Which OCR model does this use?

Baidu’s open-source PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5, running in-browser via ONNX Runtime.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Only model weights are downloaded once and cached. Your image stays on-device.

What languages are supported?

Chinese and English are strongest; many Latin-script invoices and receipts work well.
