Frequently asked questions

Can I add a watermark to every page at once?

Yes. The watermark text, color, opacity, and angle you set apply to every page of the PDF automatically.

Can I control how visible the watermark is?

Yes, use the opacity slider — low values create a subtle background stamp, higher values make it bold and obvious.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Watermarking uses pdf-lib entirely inside your browser — your file never leaves your device.
