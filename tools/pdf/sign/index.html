Frequently asked questions

How do I sign a PDF for free online?

Upload your PDF, click Signature, then draw, type, or upload your signature image. Drag it into place, resize if needed, then download the signed PDF.

Is my signature saved anywhere?

Only in your own browser's local storage, if you check "Save this signature" — it never leaves your device or gets uploaded anywhere. You can clear it anytime.

Does signing rasterize or blur my PDF?

No. Your original PDF content stays untouched and selectable — the signature, initials, and date are added on top using vector overlays, not a re-rendered image of the page.

Is this a legally binding signature?

This creates a visual e-signature suitable for many everyday documents. For contracts requiring certified or notarized signatures, check your local legal requirements.
