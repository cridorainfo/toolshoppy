Frequently asked questions

Can I reorder PDF pages online for free?

Yes. Upload a PDF, drag any page thumbnail to a new position (or use the arrow buttons on mobile), then save. Everything runs in your browser.

Can I delete or rotate individual pages?

Yes. Each page thumbnail has a rotate button (90° per tap) and a delete button. Changes preview instantly before you save.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Organizing uses pdf-lib and PDF.js entirely inside your browser — your file never leaves your device.
