Frequently asked questions

Can I rotate just one page instead of the whole PDF?

Yes. Rotate the whole document at once with Rotate all, or tap the rotate button on any individual page thumbnail.

Does rotating reduce PDF quality?

No. Rotation only changes the page orientation metadata — the underlying content is untouched, so there's no quality loss.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Rotating uses pdf-lib and PDF.js entirely inside your browser — your file never leaves your device.
