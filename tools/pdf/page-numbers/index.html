Frequently asked questions

Can I start numbering from a page other than 1?

Yes. Set any starting number — useful when this PDF continues from another document.

Can I choose where the page number appears?

Yes. Pick bottom-left, bottom-center, bottom-right, or top-right placement before applying.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Numbering uses pdf-lib entirely inside your browser — your file never leaves your device.
