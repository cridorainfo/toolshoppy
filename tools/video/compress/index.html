Frequently asked questions

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. Compression uses client-side processing (FFmpeg.wasm-style) in your browser.

Will quality drop?

Lower size usually means some quality loss. Try a milder preset if you need sharper output.

Is it free?

Yes — free, no signup.

What about very large files?

Large videos need enough device memory. If the tab crashes, try a shorter clip or a stronger device.
