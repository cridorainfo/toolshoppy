Frequently asked questions

Does this cover FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27?

Yes. The calculator is built around the FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) slabs and regime comparison shown on the page. Always confirm final figures with a CA for filing.

New regime vs old — which is better?

It depends on your deductions. Compare both regimes with your numbers here; many salaried taxpayers prefer the new regime, but itemized deductions can still favour the old regime.

Is my salary data uploaded?

No. Tax estimates run entirely in your browser. We do not collect income figures.

Is this an official Income Tax Department tool?

No. ToolShoppy is an independent free calculator for estimates only — use the official portal for filing returns.
