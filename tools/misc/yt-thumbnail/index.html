Choose the available thumbnail size

Enter a video URL or ID and inspect the available previews. A video may not have every resolution; an unavailable preview does not necessarily mean the video link is invalid. Use the largest available image that fits your intended layout.

Thumbnails are retrieved from YouTube’s image service, so loading them requires a network connection. Downloading a preview does not grant reuse rights: use your own artwork or obtain the creator’s permission before publishing it elsewhere. Keep the original aspect ratio to avoid stretching the image.

How to download a YouTube thumbnail

  1. Paste the YouTube video URL.
  2. Choose available thumbnail resolutions.
  3. Download the image.

How YouTube Thumbnail Grabber works

You paste a YouTube link or video ID and the page works out the video ID, then requests the public thumbnail images that YouTube’s image service offers in several sizes. It shows the sizes that exist and lets you download one.

It does not download the video. Not every video has every size, so a missing size does not mean the link is wrong. Thumbnails belong to their owners, so reuse needs permission or a valid licence.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Use the largest available size for design work.
  • Keep the original aspect ratio to avoid stretching.
  • Do not republish other creators’ thumbnails without permission.
  • Private or removed videos have no public thumbnail.
