Gold rate in Kasaragod

Kasaragod is Kerala's northernmost, most multilingual district (Malayalam, Kannada and Tulu), bordering Karnataka's own active gold market.

Jewellers in Kasaragod price 22K gold jewellery based on the same national MCX (Multi Commodity Exchange) benchmark used across Kerala. The 22K rate shown above is what most families in Kasaragod reference before visiting local jewellers. Making charges, GST, and design premiums are added on top of the base rate.

22K gold is the standard for wedding jewellery — thali chains, bangles, and kasu mala — across Kerala including Kasaragod. 24K is preferred for gold coins and sovereign (8g) investment purchases. Use our full gold rate tool to switch between Kerala, India, and UAE rates.
