About the Dubai gold rate

Dubai is one of the world's largest retail gold markets, and the Dubai Gold Souk sets the tone for UAE jewellery pricing. Gold is a global commodity priced in USD per troy ounce; UAE retailers convert that international rate to AED and revise displayed prices once or twice a day.

22K vs 24K — 24K (99.9% pure) is mainly used for bars, coins and investment gold. 22K (91.6% pure) is the standard purity for jewellery sold across the UAE, since it holds its shape better in rings, chains and bangles.

Many NRIs in the UAE compare Dubai gold prices against Kerala and India rates before buying, since the effective cost also depends on the AED→INR exchange rate at the time of purchase or remittance.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the exact jewellery shop rate?

No — this is an indicative benchmark price. Dubai jewellers add their own making charges and a small spread on top of the base gold rate, so the till price is usually a little higher.

What is the difference between 24K and 22K gold in Dubai?

24K is 99.9% pure gold and is mainly used for bars and coins. 22K is 91.6% pure (22 of 24 parts gold) and is the standard for most jewellery sold in the Dubai Gold Souk and UAE malls, since pure 24K is too soft for daily-wear settings.

How often does the UAE gold rate change?

Gold is priced globally in USD per ounce and moves throughout the trading day. Dubai retail rates are typically revised once or twice daily to track that international benchmark, converted to AED.
