How to read the Dubai gold rate

Gold is priced internationally in US dollars per troy ounce (31.1035 g). The UAE dirham is pegged to the dollar at 3.6725, so the AED price of gold moves almost exactly with the dollar price. That is why the Dubai rate on this page changes through the day but rarely jumps for currency reasons alone.

24K vs 22K vs 18K. 24K is 99.9% gold and is what bars and coins are made of. 22K (91.6%) is the usual purity for jewellery in the UAE and India, because pure gold is too soft for rings and chains. 18K (75%) is common in diamond and fashion jewellery. The 22K rate on this page is derived from the same purity ratios, so it is a per-gram reference, not a price list.

The page shows a retail reference taken from a published Dubai retail board, and puts the international spot price next to it, so you can see the retail premium over spot. Where the retail feed is unavailable, the page says so and falls back to the spot-based figure instead of silently showing something else.

How to use today’s rate when buying gold in Dubai

  1. Note the 22K or 24K per-gram reference above and the time it was last updated.
  2. Ask each shop for a written breakdown: the gold rate it uses, the weight, the making charge (per gram or a percentage) and VAT.
  3. Compare shops on the total price per gram after making charges, not on the gold rate alone. A lower headline rate with a higher making charge can cost more.
  4. If you plan to take gold to India, check the current customs allowance and duty rules from an official source before you travel, and keep your invoices.

Buying gold in the UAE: things worth knowing

Making charges, not the gold rate, are where shops differ most, and they are usually negotiable, especially on plain designs and larger purchases. Stone-set and intricate pieces carry higher charges and are priced differently when you sell them back.

Investment gold (bars and coins of 99%+ purity) and jewellery are treated differently for VAT in the UAE, and buy-back terms differ by shop. Ask for the buy-back policy and the hallmark or assay details in writing. For tax questions, use the UAE Federal Tax Authority’s guidance rather than a shop’s summary.

If you are comparing with India, convert with the AED to INR converter and remember that Indian prices include import duty and GST, so a straight conversion of the dirham price is only a rough guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the exact price at the Dubai Gold Souk?

No. It is an indicative reference. Shops add making charges and VAT and set their own spread, so the price on your invoice will be higher than the per-gram rate shown here.

Why does 22K cost less per gram than 24K?

22K contains 22 parts gold in 24 (about 91.6%), 24K contains about 99.9%. The 22K per-gram rate is roughly 22/24 of the 24K rate before making charges.

How often does the UAE gold rate change?

Gold trades almost around the clock, so the international price moves constantly. This page refreshes its rates every few minutes and shows the time of the last update; retail boards are typically revised once or twice a day.

Why is the AED gold price so close to the dollar price?

The dirham is pegged to the US dollar at 3.6725 AED per USD, so converting a dollar gold price into dirhams uses a fixed rate.
