About AED to INR

AED to INR is one of the most searched currency pairs in the Gulf, since millions of Indians working in the UAE send money home every month. The UAE Dirham (AED) is pegged to the US Dollar, so AED→INR moves in step with USD→INR.

The rate shown here is an indicative mid-market figure, useful for quick estimation before you compare remittance apps, exchange houses, or your bank's live transfer rate — providers typically add a 1-2% spread plus a fixed fee.
