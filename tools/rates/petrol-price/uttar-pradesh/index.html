Petrol price in Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh fuel prices are revised daily by Indian Oil, BPCL, and HPCL based on international crude oil rates and the rupee-dollar exchange rate. Petrol and diesel prices are uniform across all Uttar Pradesh cities including Lucknow, Kanpur, and Noida.

Uttar Pradesh's state VAT on petrol is among the factors that influence the final pump price, alongside dealer commission and freight cost. Commuters and fleet operators across the state track daily rate changes to plan fuel expenses. Use our full petrol price tool to compare Uttar Pradesh with other Indian states.

Diesel prices affect Uttar Pradesh's transport and logistics industries significantly, since most freight and public transport in India runs on diesel. Bookmark this page for daily Uttar Pradesh fuel rate updates.
