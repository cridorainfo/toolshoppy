Petrol price in Karnataka

Karnataka fuel prices are revised daily by Indian Oil, BPCL, and HPCL based on international crude oil rates and the rupee-dollar exchange rate. Petrol and diesel prices are uniform across all Karnataka cities including Bengaluru, Mysuru, and Mangaluru.

Karnataka's state VAT on petrol is among the factors that influence the final pump price, alongside dealer commission and freight cost. Commuters and fleet operators across the state track daily rate changes to plan fuel expenses. Use our full petrol price tool to compare Karnataka with other Indian states.

Diesel prices affect Karnataka's transport and logistics industries significantly, since most freight and public transport in India runs on diesel. Bookmark this page for daily Karnataka fuel rate updates.
