Silver rate in Kasaragod

Kasaragod is Kerala's northernmost, most multilingual district (Malayalam, Kannada and Tulu), bordering Karnataka's own active silver market.

Silver in Kasaragod is priced off the same national benchmark used across Kerala, and is popular for coins, utensils, anklets (payal), and gifting on festivals and weddings. Making charges are added on top of the base rate shown above.

Frequently asked questions

What is the silver rate in Kasaragod today?

Kasaragod silver rates track the same statewide reference used across Kerala. This page shows today's per-gram, per-10g and per-kg rates, updated daily.

Does silver cost differently in Kasaragod than the rest of Kerala?

The base rate is set at the state level and is the same across Kerala districts, including Kasaragod. What varies locally is jewellers' making charges added on top.
