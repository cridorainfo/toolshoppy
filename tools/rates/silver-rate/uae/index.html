What the UAE silver rate on this page means

Silver trades internationally in US dollars per troy ounce. Because the dirham is pegged to the dollar at 3.6725, the page converts the spot price to AED and shows it per gram, per 10 grams and per kilogram, the units that bullion dealers and jewellers in the UAE actually quote.

This is a spot-based reference, and the page labels it that way. What you pay in a shop is higher, because it includes the dealer’s premium, making charges for jewellery and VAT. Coins and bars are usually closer to spot than jewellery; heavily worked pieces can cost several times the metal value.

Silver is far more volatile than gold in percentage terms and has large industrial demand (electronics, solar panels), so its price can move sharply in a day. Use the figure for orientation and ask for a written quote before you buy or sell.

How to compare a silver quote in the UAE

  1. Read the per-gram spot reference above and note the update time.
  2. Ask the dealer for the metal price, premium or making charge, and VAT as separate lines.
  3. Divide the total by the weight to get the real price per gram, and compare it to the spot reference to see the markup.
  4. For jewellery, check the purity stamp: 925 means sterling silver (92.5% silver), 999 is fine silver.

Sterling vs fine silver, and what affects the price

Fine silver (999) is soft and is used for bars, coins and some ritual items. Sterling silver (925) is alloyed, usually with copper, for strength, and is the standard for jewellery and cutlery. A 925 item contains 92.5% of its weight in silver, so its metal value per gram is lower than the per-gram rate shown for pure silver.

The spot price follows global demand, the US dollar, interest rates and investor flows. Local factors in the UAE are the dealer premium and the purchase-side VAT treatment, which can differ between investment silver and jewellery, so check the invoice rather than assuming.

If you are comparing with India, silver prices there include import duty and GST, so a direct conversion with the AED to INR converter will understate the rupee price you see in Indian shops.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the price I will pay at a silver shop in Dubai?

No. It is the international spot price converted to AED. Shops add a premium, making charges and VAT, so retail prices are higher.

What is the difference between 925 and 999 silver?

925 (sterling) is 92.5% silver alloyed with other metals for hardness and is used for jewellery. 999 is 99.9% pure silver, used for bars and coins.

Why does the page show per gram, per 10 grams and per kilogram?

Different buyers use different units: individuals usually think in grams, small dealers in 10 g, bullion dealers in kilograms.

How often is the silver rate updated?

The spot feed refreshes every few minutes and the page shows the time of the last update.

Where can I check the gold rate in the UAE?
