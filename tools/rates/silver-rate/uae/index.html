About the UAE silver rate

Silver is priced globally in USD per troy ounce alongside gold, and UAE retailers convert that benchmark to AED for the local market. Dubai's Gold & Silver Souk is the main reference point for retail silver pricing in the Emirates.

Most silver jewellery sold in the UAE is 925 sterling silver (92.5% pure, alloyed for strength), while 999 fine silver bars and coins are closer to pure. Investors and NRIs often track this page alongside the Dubai gold rate and the AED→INR rate before buying or remitting.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the Dubai souk rate?

It is an indicative benchmark figure for estimation, not a binding quote — jewellers and bullion dealers in the Dubai Gold & Silver Souk add their own making charges and spread.

Why does UAE silver price move daily?

Silver, like gold, is priced globally in USD per ounce and fluctuates with international commodity markets. UAE retailers convert that rate to AED and update prices once or twice a day.

Is 999 silver different from 925 sterling silver?

Yes. 999 (fine silver) is 99.9% pure and used for bars and coins. 925 sterling silver is 92.5% pure, alloyed for durability, and is what most silver jewellery in the UAE is made from.
